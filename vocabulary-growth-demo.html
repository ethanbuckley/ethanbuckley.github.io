Interactive · Vocabulary growth

How many words children with Down syndrome understand, say and sign at each age

These estimates come from a pooled Bayesian model fitted to 1,424 parent-report assessments from 14 datasets in six countries. Move the age slider to read them at any age from 8 months to 6 years, or switch to the production ratio, the share of understood words a child also says.

The curves and shaded bands are the fitted model’s estimates (model fit of 4 September 2026; findings preliminary). The band-width slider is an illustrative what-if. It widens or narrows the bands on screen and does not refit the model.

Companion to the vocabulary growth study. This page uses no trackers.
