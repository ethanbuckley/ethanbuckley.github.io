Down Syndrome Education International · Reading & Language (RLI) Study

A causal design for reading progress

Separating what the teaching causes from what merely correlates with a child’s general ability.

How to read it
Randomised effect, causal
Hypothesised skill pathway, association
General-ability confounding
The identification

Randomisation makes the intervention independent of general ability, so its effect on word reading is read as genuine cause. Every skill-to-skill link stays confounded by ability, so it is reported as association, not cause.
