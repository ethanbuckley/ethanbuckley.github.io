Effect of the reading intervention on each skill

Intention-to-treat estimates on the log-odds scale with 89% equal-tailed intervals; 54 children randomised to the teaching or a waiting list. Right of zero favours the teaching. Blue intervals exclude zero; grey intervals span it.

Source: Down Syndrome Education International, Reading and Language Intervention trial, Bayesian re-analysis; single-outcome models, reporting-tier fits of 21 July 2026. Randomisation identifies these effects as causal; effects on broad vocabulary are not distinguishable from zero. Preliminary.
