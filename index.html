Independent project · live

SwimSignal

Problem
Existing maps show sewage spills that are happening or have already happened. I wanted a forecast a swimmer could check a few days before a river or lake swim in England.
What I did
I designed and run it: a model that predicts storm-overflow spills, a river model that carries them downstream, and a site that scores its own forecasts in public. The code was written with AI assistants under my direction.
Result
On held-out 2025 United Utilities data, the spill model’s Brier score was 0.0445, against 0.0669 for each overflow’s historical spill rate. Lower is better.

DSE internship · research

Vocabulary growth in children with Down syndrome

Problem
Parent-report vocabulary checklists came from 14 datasets in six countries, and the checklists differ in length. The study needed one model of how understanding and speaking develop.
What I did
Within my supervisor’s framework, I built the signing models, the comparison models for typically developing children and a within-child model.
Result
The models are now part of the study’s analysis. I rebuilt the data preparation as tested scripts feeding one DuckDB view, and drafted the report’s data chapter. The study’s findings are not final yet.

DSE internship · software

Software for teaching materials

Problem
An early-vocabulary teaching programme needed thousands of images, audio recordings and video clips, each one checked and traceable to a catalogue record.
What I did
I built the image and audio pipelines, including a blind listening test to compare voices, set up the video workflow, and wrote the audit and catalogue software.
Result
More than 7,000 teaching images, audio for about 3,400 curriculum items in four accents, and more than 23,000 draft audio files reconciled with the catalogue.
